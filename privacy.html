TabSpaces — Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary

TabSpaces has no servers. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of your data.

What the extension stores

The titles and addresses of the tabs and links you save, how you organise them (categories, spaces, tab groups), their change history, and your settings. All of this is stored locally in your browser.

Google sign-in (optional)

  • Google Drive (drive.file): used only to keep your spaces in sync between your computers and to keep backup copies. TabSpaces can only access the files it creates itself, in a TabSpaces folder of your own Drive; it cannot see any other file.
  • Gmail (gmail.send, only if you turn on e-mail alerts): used only to send alerts about your own data (for example a failed backup or many tabs removed at once) from your account to the address you choose. TabSpaces cannot read, list or delete your e-mail.
  • Your Google account e-mail address is shown in the settings so you know which account is connected.

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide these features to you. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. TabSpaces' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party services. The only network requests go to Google's APIs on your behalf.

Your control

  • Sign out in TabSpaces settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the TabSpaces folder in your Drive to remove the synced copies.
  • Removing the extension deletes the data stored in your browser.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
